Model Selection Rationale

The best model is selected based on Macro F1-Score rather than accuracy alone, because:

  • Accuracy can be misleading with imbalanced classes (e.g. more positive than negative reviews).
  • Macro F1 treats all classes equally, penalising models that perform poorly on minority classes.
  • Precision vs. Recall tradeoff is considered via the F1 harmonic mean.
  • Cross-validation was used during training to prevent overfitting on the test set.
